# Code of Conduct

Loomrail is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- discuss ideas and code without attacking people;
- assume good intent while still giving direct, evidence-based feedback;
- respect privacy, boundaries, and differences in experience or background;
- keep security reports, credentials, customer data, and other sensitive material out of public discussions;
- accept maintainer decisions on scope, moderation, and repository safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another
person's private information without permission are not tolerated in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an immediate GitHub-hosted incident, use GitHub's built-in block and report tools. To contact the Loomrail
maintainers privately, use the repository's
[private reporting channel](https://github.com/loomrail/loomrail/security/advisories/new) and mark the report as a
conduct issue rather than a vulnerability.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when needed
to protect the community. Reports will be reviewed privately, without retaliation, and with only the disclosure
needed to investigate and enforce this policy.
