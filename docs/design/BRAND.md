# Loomrail brand

Loomrail is the local control plane for accountable AI software teams. The identity should feel operational,
technical, and calm: compact neutral surfaces, explicit state, and one indigo signal color.

## Mark

The mark is built from two nested rails that form an `L`. The outer route represents the delivery pipeline; the
inner route represents parallel agent work kept inside the same accountable path.

- Primary accent: `#5e6ad2`
- Near black: `#1b1b1d`
- Light canvas: `#f9f9fa`
- Dark canvas: `#171719`
- Minimum digital size: 20 px
- Clear space: at least one quarter of the mark width on every side

Do not add gradients, glows, shadows, outlines, or alternate colors to the mark. Do not rotate, stretch, crop, or
redraw its rails. Use the mark alone for compact product surfaces and the wordmark for repository or editorial use.
